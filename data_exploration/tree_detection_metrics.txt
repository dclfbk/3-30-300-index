3. PERFORMANCE METRICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Precision  =  TP / (TP + FP)  =  52 / 60  =  0.867   →  86.7%
  Recall     =  TP / (TP + FN)  =  52 / 78  =  0.667   →  66.7%
  F1-score   =  2 × P × R / (P + R)          =  0.754   →  75.4%

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. INTERPRETATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Precision 86.7%
    When the model detects a tree, it is correct 87% of the time.
    Very low false alarm rate (only 8 FP across 78 reference trees).

  Recall 66.7%
    The model successfully finds 2 out of every 3 real trees.
    Approximately one third of trees are missed (FN = 26).

  F1-score 75.4%
    Reasonable overall performance for a zero-shot model with no
    fine-tuning on Italian urban tree data.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. LIMITATIONS & NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Zero-shot inference: DeepForest was not fine-tuned on Bologna data.
    Performance is expected to improve with domain-specific fine-tuning.

  - Low recall is characteristic of zero-shot DeepForest behaviour:
    the model tends to miss smaller crowns and trees partially obscured
    by building shadows or adjacent canopies.
