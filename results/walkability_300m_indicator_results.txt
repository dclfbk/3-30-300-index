AR_VRD FILTERING 
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- Method: count of building footprints intersecting each park polygon
- Threshold chosen: ≤ 6 buildings (choosen via QGIS visual inspection ... )
- Removed: 1,579 polygons (41.4% of ar_vrd)
- Rationale: ar_vrd polygons with >6 intersecting footprints were identified as
  residential block interiors rather than public green spaces

LIMITATIONS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
- 2,680 parks excluded — no OSM walkable edge intersects boundary
  (likely private/inaccessible)
- ar_vrd threshold of ≤6 buildings empirically chosen, not literature-derived
  — sensitivity table provided above
- Address points are civic addresses, not building centroids or façade points - can be hard to get simular dataset from different city
