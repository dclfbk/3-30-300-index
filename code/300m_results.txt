- Street network: OSM via osmnx, `network_type="walk"` (66,622 edges)

ar_vrd Filtering
- Method: count of building footprints intersecting each park polygon
- Threshold chosen: ≤ 6 buildings (empirically determined via QGIS visual inspection)
- Removed: 1,579 polygons (41.4% of ar_vrd)
- Rationale: ar_vrd polygons with >6 intersecting footprints were identified as residential block interiors rather than public green spaces

Distribution of buildings inside ar_vrd polygons: mean 10.5, median 4.0, max 201.

Park Entrance Detection
- Raw entrance points: 10,246
- Parks with no walkable entrance: 2,680 (70% of 3,807)
- Entrance points after 20m deduplication: 3,528

Routing — Isochrone Method
- One 300m walking isochrone per entrance point (3,528 GraphHopper queries)
- Buildings matched via spatial join (point-in-polygon)

Limitations
- 2,680 parks excluded — no OSM walkable edge intersects boundary (likely private/inaccessible)
- 20m deduplication threshold not backed by literature
- ar_vrd threshold of ≤6 buildings empirically chosen, not literature-derived — sensitivity table provided above
- Address points are civic addresses, not building centroids or façade points
- Isochrone method gives binary access (yes/no within 300m), not exact walking distance
